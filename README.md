## India's Quick-Commerce Market: Secondary Research

## Project Overview
This project explores India's quick-commerce market, focusing on market trends, competitive positioning, consumer and category developments, and key business challenges.

## Research Objectives
- Understand the growth drivers and emerging trends in India's quick-commerce industry.
- Compare major players such as Blinkit, Swiggy Instamart, and Zepto.
- Identify key competitive differences, challenges, and potential growth opportunities.

## Research Approach
This report uses secondary research based on publicly available industry reports, company disclosures, and credible business news sources. The findings are synthesized to develop market observations and business insights.

## Key Areas Covered
- Market growth and industry trends
- Competitor comparison
- Business models and competitive positioning
- Profitability, operational challenges, and growth opportunities
- Key takeaways and recommendations

## Tools and Skills
Secondary Research | Competitor Analysis | Trend Analysis | Excel | Data Interpretation | Business Writing

## Scope and Limitations
This is an independent, AI-assisted portfolio project based on public sources. No primary interviews or surveys were conducted. Market estimates and interpretations should be read in the context of their cited sources and publication dates.

## Disclaimer
This project was prepared for learning and portfolio purposes. It is not commissioned research and is not affiliated with any company mentioned in the report.
